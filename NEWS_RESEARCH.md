### 1. GTA VI Launches Worldwide
Grand Theft Auto VI releases globally on 20 November 2026. 15.9 million
pre-orders banked; $79.99 on PS5 and Xbox (no PC at launch). Scored 99/100
on Metacritic, tying the highest score in history. New Zealand unlocks first

### 2. Cyclone Akvan Makes Landfall in Odisha
Severe Cyclonic Storm Akvan crossed the North Andhra Pradesh–South Odisha
coast near Gopalpur on the morning of 20 November. 300,000+ people evacuated
to 6,000+ shelters. Sustained winds 70–80 kmph, gusts to 90–110 kmph.
39 trains cancelled, 28 rescheduled. Red alert in multiple districts.
Sources: India Meteorological Department, The Hindu, India Today, Indian Express.

### 3. COP31 Enters Final Day Without a Deal
The UN climate summit's final scheduled day in Antalya, Türkiye. The revised
cover decision stripped fossil-fuel language and the $120bn adaptation-finance
target. French minister called the omission a "black mark". Nearly 200 nations
divided; talks may spill into the weekend.
Sources: AP News, AFP, UN News, The Guardian, The Hindu.
