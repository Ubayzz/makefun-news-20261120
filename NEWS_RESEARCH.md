Verified world news research for **Friday, 20 November 2026**.

## Lead Stories

### 1. GTA VI Global Launch
Rockstar Games releases Grand Theft Auto VI worldwide on 20 November 2026 — the most
anticipated entertainment launch of the year. 15.9 million pre-orders were banked ahead
of release day. The game ships for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S at $79.99 (no PC
version at launch). Metacritic aggregates a 99/100 score, tying the all-time record.
New Zealand unlocks first at local midnight due to time zone. Pre-order bonuses include
the "Vintage Vice City Pack" and one month of GTA+ subscription.

### 2. Cyclone Akvan Strikes Odisha Coast
Severe Cyclonic Storm Akvan crossed the North Andhra Pradesh – South Odisha coastline
near Gopalpur during Friday morning. Over 300,000 residents were evacuated to more than
6,000 relief shelters ahead of landfall. The India Meteorological Department reported
sustained winds of 70–80 km/h with gusts reaching 90–110 km/h. Indian Railways cancelled
39 trains and rescheduled 28 others. A red alert was declared across multiple coastal
districts.

### 3. COP31 Enters Final Scheduled Day Without Agreement
The UN climate summit in Antalya, Türkiye reaches its last scheduled day with no deal
in sight. A revised draft cover decision removed references to fossil fuel phase-out and
dropped the $120 billion adaptation-finance target. France's minister described the
omission as a "black mark" on the negotiations. Nearly 200 nations remain divided;
discussions are expected to continue into the weekend.

## Secondary Stories

**Berlin Coalition Summit** — Over 40 nations endorsed a four-party negotiation format
(Ukraine, Europe, United States, Russia). The United Kingdom pledged £260 million in
winter humanitarian aid and committed 1,000 long-range strike drones.

**Crimean Bridge Attacked Again** — A third strike targeted the Kerch Strait bridge.
President Putin ruled out ceasefire talks and announced a free Kerch–Kavkaz ferry service
starting 20 November.

**Anthropic IPO Breaks Records** — Anthropic (ticker: ANT) debuted on Nasdaq with an
$81.3 billion raise — the largest IPO in history. The stock closed up 17% on day one,
with the company's valuation briefly approaching $2 trillion. Over 846 million shares
changed hands.

**Trump–Vance Rift** — Former President Trump expressed frustration with JD Vance over
Iran-war messaging following the midterm elections, while praising Marco Rubio as a
potential 2028 Republican candidate.

**Gaza Ground Operation** — Israeli armour advanced near the Nuseirat refugee camp beyond
the established Yellow Line. Approximately 6,000 people were displaced. The IDF stated
it dismantled a tunnel complex during the operation.

**IEA Oil Supply Warning** — The International Energy Agency estimated the Middle East
conflict has cost 2.8 billion barrels in oil supply. The report noted that global
reliance on the Strait of Hormuz transit route may decline permanently as buyers
diversify.

**Market Slide** — The Dow Jones Industrial Average closed below 50,000 and the Nasdaq
Composite fell 2%. AI-sector enthusiasm waned despite the record Anthropic debut.
